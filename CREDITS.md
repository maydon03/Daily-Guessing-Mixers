# Credits

Daily Queue is an independent fan project inspired by daily deduction and word games. It is not an official Wordle, LoLdle, or Pokédle product.

## League of Legends

Champion names, titles, classifications, base statistics, and portraits: Riot Games Data Dragon, version 16.20.1. Retrieved October 8, 2026.

- Documentation: https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol
- Dataset: https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.20.1/data/en_US/champion.json
- Portraits: the corresponding Data Dragon champion image files.

Daily Queue isn't endorsed by Riot Games and doesn't reflect the views or opinions of Riot Games or anyone officially involved in producing or managing League of Legends. League of Legends and Riot Games are trademarks or registered trademarks of Riot Games, Inc.

- https://pokeapi.co/

## English dictionary

Accepted guesses: five-letter entries from dwyl/english-words, plus a separately curated answer list. Its source license is included in `LICENSES/english-words.md`.

- https://github.com/dwyl/english-words

These characters, names, and artwork remain owned by Riot Games, Nintendo, Game Freak, and The Pokémon Company respectively.
